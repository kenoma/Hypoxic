I developed this app as a side project many years ago while working on my PhD thesis. Recently, however, it has become unavailable from app stores for reasons beyond my control. 

The app provides a wide range of traditional and non-traditional breathing exercises. You can customise training modes, visualise the process, and set audio signals to transition between phases.

It also includes a training diary which is stored locally on your device.
